Welcome, seal keeper!

A tiny egg has washed up on the shore. Keep it warm, then feed, play with, and clean your seal to help it grow. When it's fully grown, it'll swim back to the sea and leave you a gift — and a brand new egg to raise, a little better than before.

How to play 🦭

🥚 Egg: Tap "Warm the Egg" to help it hatch.

🐟 Hunger / 🎾 Happiness / 💤 Energy / 🧼 Clean / ❤️ Health all drift down over time — tend to them or your seal may fall sick.

Growth fills as you care for your seal well. Fill it fully at each stage to evolve: Pup → Juvenile → Adult.

🌊 Return to the Sea: once fully grown and healthy, your seal returns home, leaving you a random gift and a brand new — slightly stronger — egg.

🏊 👋 🌀 Tank actions: use the little buttons beside the tank to make your seal swim a lap, wave hello, or spin — it'll talk to you too! Tap the seal directly for a quick poke.

Earn 🐚 shells by playing mini-games and beachcombing over time. Spend them in the Shop.
